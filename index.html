How I Sell

From enquiry to confident decision.

A repeatable sales process that keeps the customer at the centre.

01 — DISCOVERY

Understand the homeowner.

Energy usage, bills, property, goals, pain points and what triggered the enquiry.

02 — QUALIFICATION

Find the real problem.

Fit, urgency, budget, decision process and reason behind the purchase.

03 — SOLUTION

Recommend the right system.

Solar, inverter and battery configuration aligned with the customer's situation.

04 — ROI

Make the numbers clear.

Savings, tariffs, self-consumption and payback explained simply.

05 — OBJECTIONS

Protect the value.

Work through price, finance, battery, installation and alternative-solution concerns.

06 — CLOSE

Make the next step easy.

Clarify the proposal, confirm value and confidently ask for the decision.
